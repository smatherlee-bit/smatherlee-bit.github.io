Mat-Nest Pathfinder

Mat-Nest Pathfinder is a private, personal travel tool used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it does: it reads travel booking emails (flights, trains, hotels, car rentals) that the owner has placed under one Gmail label, and turns them into trip entries in the owner's own self-hosted trip planner.

  • Access is read-only (Gmail scope gmail.readonly). It never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Only messages under the owner's chosen label are read.
  • Everything runs on the owner's own home server. No data is sold, shared or sent to advertisers.
